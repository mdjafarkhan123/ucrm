## Notes

- Teammates are a separate login from contractor accounts: their own table and password, the same
  `jafar_session` cookie and session registry as Jafar, so removing one ends access on the next request.
- Starting access, "careful start" (Jafar, 2026-10-07): Sales — Leads (work) and Applications (look only);
  Delivery — Onboarding (look only); Support — Support inbox (read and reply); Platform Operations —
  Organizations, Packages, Operations (look only). Overview, Settings, Client setup, Team, payment,
  provisioning, package changes, and provider controls stay Jafar's until D2's switches.
- Password at least 8 characters, at most 72, as contractor logins (Jafar, 2026-10-07).
- Defaults copied from existing flows: invitation link lasts 7 days (contractor invites); sign-in lasts 8
  hours (Jafar's); reset link lasts 1 hour and is single-use; a reset signs out the teammate's other sessions.
