# Boulevard P3 — first-release boundary and clinic readiness

**Status:** P3 release boundary agreed by Jafar, 2026-10-08. This family-level scope is not a promise of identical Boulevard feature parity or a claim that any new-edition capability is built. The [public inventory](boulevard-feature-landscape-2026-10-05.md) describes Boulevard, the [product plan](../boulevard-product-behavior-contract.md) describes agreed behavior, and the [feasibility checkpoint](boulevard-p3-feasibility-2026-10-07.md) describes current reuse and provider limits.

## Agreed release boundary

Launch one complete, single-location Medspa & Clinical Wellness journey for a clinic whose actual services and old obligations can be supported. The first clinic **may be an existing business**. Select it after reviewing its treatments, state, age groups, future bookings, outstanding paid value, source files, and stock practice. Do not assume every clinic is new, adult-only, or free of memberships. A clinic that depends on a capability still awaiting verification waits for that capability or uses an explicitly reviewed outside process where the agreed plan permits one. The product must show those limits honestly.

This boundary follows Boulevard's connected booking, clinical-record, checkout, and medspa-package pattern. [Boulevard's package guidance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15117321-packages-best-practices) makes prepaid benefits a real medspa need. [Zenoti's batch and expiry guidance](https://help.zenoti.com/en/inventory/operational-tasks/batch-and-expiry-management-in-inventory.html) and [Aesthetic Record's procedure trace](https://learn.aestheticrecord.com/en/articles/9247099-add-traceability-information-to-a-procedure) inform the stock gate. These release stages and safeguards are our product decisions, not a claim about Boulevard's release stages.

## First-clinic eligibility and journey proof

Before offering a clinic a go-live date, record its state and services; who treats and reviews; age groups and guardian needs; whether it prescribes; appointment and resource rules; card/deposit policy; current packages, memberships, gift cards, units and outstanding balances; future bookings; source data and clinical files; retail and injectable stock practice; and required connections. Match each item to a verified capability or an allowed, documented outside process. In particular:

1. **Clinical and state gate:** clinic policy and the states served are reviewed for consent, minors, access, retention, gift cards and paid-value terms. Required provider agreements and safeguards for protected information are actually in place. A clinic treating minors is accepted only after the approved representative and confidentiality journey is verified for that state. A clinic prescribing through the product waits for the qualified provider integration; outside prescribing must remain clearly outside the product.
2. **Money and migration gate:** clinic-owned Stripe permissions, saved-card consent if required, refunds and payout traces pass a test. Old client matches, future visits and every outstanding paid-benefit type reconcile against source totals. If a needed type cannot be migrated or honored, migration is incomplete and the clinic does not switch that journey.
3. **Treatment and stock gate:** each offered treatment has its age, prerequisite, clearance and chart rules configured. If injectable stock is external, the clinic verifies who records lot, expiry, quantity, person, visit, wastage and recall lookup there. No platform stock claim is made.
4. **Connected journey:** a website booking reaches the right person and service; forms and consent remain on that person's record; an authorized clinician clears and charts; checkout applies the correct deposit and benefit; a receipt and daily report reconcile; a failed message, payment or outside connection leaves visible work for staff.
5. **Setup and access:** staff can finish the pinned medspa Setup program, see only purchased features, and exercise their own permissions. A new program publication and a suspended staff account do not alter the first clinic's history or leave a live clinical session behind.

This is an **eligibility review**, not a guarantee that the current application is ready. The [feasibility checkpoint](boulevard-p3-feasibility-2026-10-07.md) found reuse candidates, not completed Medspa capabilities. P3A still has to set workloads and measurements before any capacity or speed promise.

## Decisions agreed with Jafar, 2026-10-08

1. The **core journey and clinic-dependent rule** are approved. Select the first clinic against the readiness checklist; no blanket new-clinic/adult/no-benefits assumption applies.
2. **One-time packages and service vouchers** are mandatory first-release capabilities. Recurring memberships remain clinic-dependent until a selected clinic requires them.
3. A clinic may use **reviewed outside injectable stock tracking** while appointments, charts and money are in this product. The outside record must name the person, visit, lot, expiry, quantity and recall responsibility. The product must state that it does not manage that stock. A clinic requiring in-product stock management waits for the full trace.

Source-vendor samples, paid-unit valuation, actual provider agreements, saved-card key permissions and the first clinic's treatment list are still unverified. No P3 decision can turn these into assumed facts.
