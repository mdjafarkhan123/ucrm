## Evidence and its decision

| Question | Verified reference behavior | Inference and proposed adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization or personal choice? | Salesforce permits one active organization theme. Creating, editing, previewing and activating require its Customize Application permission; preview and activation are separate. [Considerations](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.lex_themes_and_branding_considerations.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [Activate a Theme](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.brand_activate_theme.htm&language=en_US&type=5). Slack's custom theme is visible only to its chooser; sharing colors lets another person apply them. [Slack themes](https://slack.com/intl/en-gb/help/articles/205166337-change-your-slack-theme). | Organization activation and personal customization are distinct policies. For the CRM's organization-theme goal, propose owner/admin control with private preview and explicit saving; ordinary staff use the organization's design. This is our permission adaptation, not Salesforce's role model. |
| Must light/dark follow the organization? | Slack offers light, dark and system preference; its dark-mode preference is device specific. [Slack dark mode](https://slack.com/help/articles/360019434914-Use-dark-mode-in-Slack/slack.com/). | Color mode can be a separate personal choice. Propose deciding it separately and verifying every offered theme/mode combination. Do not infer cross-device synchronization from a device preference. |
| How should appearance and layouts relate? | Atlassian describes tokens as reusable named design decisions whose values are supplied by themes, selected by meaning rather than matching a color. It separately defines layout areas for banners, top navigation, side navigation, main content and panels. [Design tokens](https://atlassian.design/foundations/tokens/design-tokens/), [Navigation layout](https://atlassian.design/components/navigation-system/layout). Salesforce separately provides page templates and component regions for record content. [Console pages](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.console_lex_custom_pages.htm&language=en_US&type=5). | Semantic style roles can share components' purpose; structural differences need explicit composition. Propose shared records, permissions and operations, with theme values and supported layout frames. This does not establish competitors' private implementation or require their component libraries. |
| Is a selector evidence of complete coverage? | Salesforce documents styling exceptions and surfaces excluded from its themes. Microsoft documents modern-theme exceptions including legacy grids, row summaries, focus view and the sales pipeline. [Salesforce considerations](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.lex_themes_and_branding_considerations.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [Microsoft modern themes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/model-driven-apps/modern-theme-overrides). | Coverage is a release decision. Propose an explicit boundary and complete normal journeys in it, including forms and overlays. Existing outer-frame support alone is insufficient. |
| Should businesses freely change colors? | Microsoft's palette generation favors accessibility; locking an exact seed color can yield insufficient contrast. It calls for checking header foreground/background contrast in each interaction state. [Microsoft modern themes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/maker/model-driven-apps/modern-theme-overrides). | Brand color and readability can conflict. Propose tested presets first; constrained custom branding would be separate scope with contrast checks. |

## Accessibility acceptance direction

WCAG 2.2 AA generally requires 4.5:1 text contrast, or 3:1 for large text, subject to exceptions; required visual information identifying controls and states generally needs 3:1 against adjacent colors. Reflow normally preserves information and functionality at 320 CSS pixels without two-dimensional scrolling, except content whose meaning requires that layout. Sequential focus preserves meaning and operability. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

Propose checking every offered design and mode with actual content, backgrounds, validation, selection, focus, overlays, phone navigation and enlarged text. A palette or preview thumbnail cannot establish whole-experience accessibility. Layout changes must keep permitted records and actions reachable.

## Switching has no single policy to copy

Salesforce documents immediate visibility after activation, while Microsoft says its app-header theme may require browser refresh. Their sources above do not establish safe unfinished-form behavior, concurrent administrator changes, unavailable assets or updates across CRM tabs.

Propose private preview, explicit organization saving, previous selection retained after a failed save, and no loss of unfinished staff work. Recommend applying changes in active sessions at a safe point with a notice rather than forcing a refresh during editing. Exact timing, rollback and default selection need agreement and implementation proof.

## Limits

This focused comparison supports product choices; it does not prove CRM readiness, complete accessibility, asset isolation or performance. Theme names and old design approvals are not research conclusions. Jafar chooses release scope, supported layouts, customization and modes; the AI chooses maintainable mechanisms within that agreement.
