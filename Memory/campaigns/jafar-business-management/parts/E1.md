## Notes

- Design (proven: Calendly/HubSpot, contractor `private.compute_form_available_slots`): one SQL function decides open slots; the book command takes a per-host advisory lock and re-runs that function for the chosen time, so two visitors can never both win. Calls and Busy blocks of the host (with buffer) hide time; timed to-dos do not. Availability is in the host's saved time zone; the visitor sees times in their browser's zone.
- Visitor gives name, email, phone, business name, country, trade, optional note. The booking joins the one Lead whose email matches exactly; otherwise it creates a Lead (source "contacted us"), owned by the host. The call becomes the Lead's next action and takes the host's default reminders.
