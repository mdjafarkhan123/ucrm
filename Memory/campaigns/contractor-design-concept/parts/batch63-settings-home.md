## Grouping (research)

- Jobber: Business management (company, products & services, payments, taxes), Team organisation (team,
  work settings, schedule, checklists), Client communication (client hub, emails & texts, requests, booking);
  marketing and connected apps apart.
- Housecall Pro: Global (company profile, hours, service area, billing, notifications, login), Feature
  configurations (estimates, invoices, jobs, price book with tax rates, pipeline, inbox, intake, marketing),
  Tags & tools (checklists, tags).
- ServiceTitan: Settings home with Setup, People (employees, role permissions, payroll), Operations.
- Our groups: **Your business** (profile, branding, hours, taxes, payments) · **Work** (quotes, invoices,
  products & services, checklists, pipeline) · **Talking to customers** (email, phone & texting, website chat,
  request forms, new-customer alerts, templates, snippets, contact matching, blocked addresses, texting
  balance) · **Team** (members, roles, activity) · **Growth** (automations, Google reviews) · **Your account**
  (photo, security, desktop alerts).
