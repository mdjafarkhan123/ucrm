# Business entry and onboarding: remaining rules

**Question:** How should an industry-specific setup program change safely, and how should an existing or mixed business be assigned an experience?

## Official-source findings

- [Boulevard's Forms and Charts builder](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/6989798-forms-and-charts-building-forms-and-charts) has a draft, desktop/mobile preview, publish and disable flow. Its [client form guidance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941366-forms-submitting-printing-saving-sending-and-deleting) distinguishes unfinished and submitted forms and keeps completed material available. These are form rules, not a published rule for changing an onboarding program while a business is partway through it.
- [Boulevard's Medspa add-on](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/9084775-medspa-add-on) can add clinical features to an existing subscription with assistance. Public guidance does not describe a complete salon-to-medspa account conversion or guarantee that all existing records safely change category.
- [Fresha's workspace creation](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/workspace-settings/40-create-a-new-workspace) asks for a primary service type and related types. [Separate Fresha workspaces](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/workspace-settings/101696-switch-between-workspaces) have separate clients, bookings, finances and permissions; that is evidence about workspaces, not an in-place edition switch.
- [Vagaro's business-type guidance](https://support.vagaro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048745274-Set-Your-Business-Type) uses listed services to decide a marketplace category. It does not establish a safe rule for clinical access or switching an application's industry experience.
- [California's medical board](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Resources/Medical-Resources/Medical-Spas.aspx) and [Texas's licensing agency](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/barbering-and-cosmetology/medical-spas.htm) describe different regulated procedures and responsible authorities. A name such as “spa” or “laser” does not reliably define the clinical boundary across states; local clinical/legal review remains necessary before release.
- [Appcues checklist testing](https://docs.appcues.com/en_US/checklists/preview-and-test-your-checklist) distinguishes a visual preview from testing actual targeting and completion. [Pendo's guide delivery](https://support.pendo.io/hc/en-us/articles/35428244709659-Test-and-deliver-your-guide) separates draft, staged and public states. Neither product documents Uplift's exact onboarding program.

## Product choices approved by Jafar 2026-10-07

1. A new published version could apply to only new businesses, leaving started businesses on their existing version unless Jafar reviews a move; this would revise the current Contractor rule that unfinished setups see new questions immediately ([Contractor onboarding plan](../client-onboarding-delivery-behavior-contract.md)). Preserve completed answers, submission history and timestamps. Map a completed task across versions only when its meaning and completion rule still match. A safety-critical change needs an explicit review of affected businesses.
2. A primary-experience change could be an owner-reviewed transition of the existing organization, with a visible impact review covering existing data, purchased capabilities, staff access, setup work and clinical readiness. If a safe transition is unsupported, defer it; never quietly recategorize an organization from its service names.
3. For ambiguous mixed businesses, ask for actual services, state, and who performs or supervises them. Hold provisioning if the clinical boundary cannot yet be determined. Confirmed regulated clinical work calls for the Medspa experience and its safeguards; this is a proposed Uplift policy, not a vendor rule.
4. A publishing journey could show the draft, exact preview for industry/business type/package and staff role, hidden and required branches, a safe test organization, and an impact summary before publishing an immutable program version. No public source establishes Boulevard's implementation of this journey.

**Evidence limit:** Jafar accepted these four recommendations as our product rules. They are drawn from public product behavior and the current application, not confirmed Boulevard onboarding behavior. Release feasibility belongs to P3.
