## Deferred within this campaign

Cut on "follow Jobber" or scoped out, not dropped. Revisit as a product decision; none blocks a planned part.

- Wire Upcoming / Today / Late / Action required into `private.job_derived_status`, `job_list_rows` and
  `job_status_count_rows` — the contract describes these derived labels; the list read models do not yet compute them.
- The standalone **Close Job** button with its own incomplete-visit preview (lifecycle commands exist; this entry point does not).
- Per-entry labor rate overrides; a start/stop timer; a company-wide Timesheets page.
- Standalone non-Job expenses; Jobber's profit alerts.
- Photo category labels — Jobber has no such field (already noted on 15b); may return as our own differentiator.
- The clients-family per-row RLS defect that 15a-4 fixed for jobs — tracked in
  `Memory/deferred/app-wide-rls-helpers-run-once-per-returned-row.md`.

Jobber evidence belongs in its domain reference and `Design/Jobber Jobs/`; proposal details belong in `docs/research/`, not Memory.
