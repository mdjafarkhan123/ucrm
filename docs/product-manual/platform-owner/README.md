# Platform Owner manual

The Platform Owner control room is the private area used to operate UpliftContractor. It is separate
from every contractor organization. Platform Owners do not enter a contractor workspace as a team
member and do not perform the contractor's everyday CRM work.

This manual will contain guides for complete and verified Platform Owner journeys, including:

- reviewing contractor applications;
- confirming offsite payments and creating organizations;
- managing packages and commercial access;
- finding organizations that need attention;
- supporting administrator access and recovery;
- handling operational failures;
- closing and restoring organizations; and
- controlling provider and integration eligibility.

Individual guides will appear here as those journeys reach their completion gates. Until then, this
page does not present unfinished behavior as available.
