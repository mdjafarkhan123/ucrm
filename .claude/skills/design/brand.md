# Brand

> Dependencies: `colors.md`

## Personality

- **Premium and calm:** polished like top Dribbble product work, quiet enough to read between jobs.
- **Contractor first:** every block answers a question a contractor has on that screen.
- **Meaningful colour:** colour names a record, a part of the app, or a status.

## Brand Colour

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-interactive` | `#037741` | `#8acc33` | Main button, current page, links |
| `--color-interactive--hover` | `#02532d` | `#a1d65c` | Hover on the above |

## Rules

- Green marks action. One green main button per area.
- Light greens appear in dark mode only.
- The business logo tile in the sidebar uses `--color-interactive` with a white letter.
