# Contractor manual

The contractor workspace helps a business manage its customers and move work from the first request
through quoting, scheduling, completion, invoicing, and payment.

This manual will grow by complete product area. Future guides may cover customers, requests, quotes,
jobs, visits, invoices, payments, team access, business settings, communications, and reporting.

Individual guides will appear here only after the related journey is available and verified in the
product.
