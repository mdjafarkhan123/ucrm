Questions waiting for Jafar (asked 2026-10-07):

1. ❓ Q1 — A business is halfway through Setup when you publish new questions. Should it finish the version it started? I recommend yes: new businesses get the new version, and an existing business moves only after you review its saved answers and unfinished tasks. This would change the current Contractor rule, where unfinished setups update immediately. Which rule do you want? Options: Keep started businesses on their version (Recommended); Update every unfinished setup immediately; Keep Contractor live updates; pin new industries.
2. ❓ Q2 — A salon already using the app starts offering medical treatments. How should its main experience change? I recommend that you review its existing records, package, staff access, setup work, and clinical readiness, then move the same business account only when those checks pass. If a safe move is unavailable, hold the change. Which approach do you want? Options: Review and move the same account (Recommended); Create a separate Medspa account; Switch automatically from its service list.
3. ❓ Q3 — A business calls itself a spa, but its services might be medical. Before creating its account, I recommend checking its actual services, US state, and who performs or supervises them. If the boundary remains unclear, hold setup until it is resolved. What should happen? Options: Check and hold if unclear (Recommended); Start it in Beauty & Spa; Start it in Medspa.
4. ❓ Q4 — Before you publish a Setup program, what should the Platform Owner check? I recommend a draft preview for the exact industry, business type, package, and staff role; a safe test account; and a summary of affected businesses before publication. Which publishing flow do you want? Options: Preview, test, then publish (Recommended); Preview only, then publish; Publish directly from the editor.
