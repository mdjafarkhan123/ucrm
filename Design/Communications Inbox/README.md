## Direction to adapt for UCRM

- Start email-only, driven by UCRM's real operational-email history.
- Use a three-pane desktop workspace: saved views/list, chronological thread, and permission-aware CRM context.
- Preserve UCRM's own visual tokens, copy, access rules, and channel-specific truth; unsupported channels are
  unavailable rather than simulated.

The current comparison and proposed redesign slices are recorded in
`docs/research/ghl-unified-inbox-ui-audit-2026-09-18.md`.
