## Decisions (Jafar, 2026-10-07)

- Each area: Off / View / Change, starting from the role. Role change asks: new role's standard access, or
  keep individual adjustments (contractor `TeamAccessEditor` pattern).
- Sensitive switches, off for every role until granted: Confirm/undo payments (confirm, reverse, correct
  package), Set up client accounts (provision, setup email), Change packages & prices (every package write;
  Packages is otherwise View-only), Client account controls (organization communications, Stripe,
  automation/chat authority, exceptions, client team edits). A switch needs its area at least View and is
  the only way to do its action.
- Managing teammates stays Jafar's only (narrows the plan; record in plan + ADR at finish).
- Actions behind Jafar's password step-up (billing, closure, lifecycle, SMS money and holds, administrator
  recovery) stay owner-only even with the switch.
- A teammate keeps at least one area; removing all access means removing them.
- History reuses `platform_audit_events` (target `platform_team_member`).
