# Boulevard P2F reporting and reconciliation research

Checked 2026-10-07 against official Boulevard and Zenoti guidance. **Confirmed** describes published product behavior, not private implementation. **Proposed** names our product rule. The newer Boulevard reporting experience is rolling out by account, and its legacy permissions can differ. Release timing and existing-app suitability belong to P3.

## Sales, payments, and payouts

| Finding | Primary evidence | Planning consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirmed:** Boulevard's Sales Summary uses closed orders and order-close dates; its payment-method widget uses payment-processing dates. Two totals for the same selected dates may therefore differ. Its Daily Summary and Sales Summary also use “Total Collected” with inconsistent descriptions. | [Sales Summary](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15591207-sales-summary-dashboard); [Daily Summary](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15591104-daily-summary-report) | **Proposed:** name each measure and its date basis; never copy the ambiguous label without a definition. |
| **Confirmed:** Order Payments lists payment and refund transactions, method, processing and settlement timing. Gift-card, credit and voucher redemption are not new bank cash. | [Order Payments](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15591116-order-payments-report); [reporting discrepancies](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/14140387-understanding-reporting-discrepancies) | **Proposed:** separate closed sales, fresh money received, previously paid value redeemed, and outstanding value. A redemption may describe a fulfilled service but is not a second card receipt. |
| **Confirmed:** Boulevard's payout report relates processor transactions to fees, adjustments, reserves, payout status and bank transfers. “Sent” is not proof of bank receipt. Stripe independently traces charges, refunds and adjustments to a payout. | [Boulevard payout reconciliation](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/16951468-understanding-payout-reconciliation-reporting); [Stripe payout reconciliation](https://support.stripe.com/questions/find-what-transactions-were-included-in-or-impacted-a-payout-amount) | **Proposed:** reconcile the processor balance to each payout and let staff confirm bank arrival; keep cash-drawer and recorded outside payments distinct. Do not copy Boulevard's processor cutoff time. |
| **Specialist pattern:** Zenoti distinguishes sales, collections and liability redemption, and explains why a prepaid purchase and later use appear differently in operating and accounting views. | [Zenoti Register Summary](https://help.zenoti.com/en/reports/finance/accounting/register-summary-v1-report.html); [Accounting Summary FAQ](https://help.zenoti.com/en/reports/finance/accounting/accounting-summary-report---faqs.html) | Show new money and care/product delivered as separately named views; do not add them into one misleading “revenue” total. |

## Prepaid value, stock, and pay

| Finding | Primary evidence | Planning consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirmed:** Boulevard publishes as-of credit, gift-card and voucher balances, plus signed credit activity. The client wallet shows purchased, used and remaining prepaid units. | [Account Credit Balance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15601602-account-credit-balance-report); [Gift Card Balance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15601609-gift-card-balance-report); [Voucher Balance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15591092-voucher-balance-report); [client wallet](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/12737024-client-profile-wallet-overview) | **Proposed:** each balance is traceable to dated issue, use, refund, expiry where lawful, and correction events. | **Gap:** No current Boulevard prepaid-product-unit monetary liability snapshot was found. Zenoti has a related prepaid-card opening/movement/closing liability report, but prepaid cards are not medspa product units. | [Boulevard prepaid-unit guide](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/8558592-usage-based-pricing-and-prepaid-units-reporting-guide); [Zenoti prepaid-card liability](https://help.zenoti.com/en/reports/finance/accounting/prepaid-cards-liability-report--v2-.html) | Track unit quantities now. ## Permissions and daily review

- **Confirmed:** Boulevard separates reporting categories, self/everyone scope and export permission, but its legacy/mobile access can bypass the newer granular categories. [Reporting Permissions](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15609508-reporting-permissions). **Proposed safeguard:** one effective access policy governs every view and export; no role receives clinical records through a financial or stock report. Zenoti separately permissions report export and allows stock views with value hidden: [report features](https://help.zenoti.com/en/reports/report-features.html), [inventory reports](https://help.zenoti.com/en/inventory/reports/inventory-reports.html).
- **Confirmed:** Boulevard's register-closeout report compares counted with expected **physical cash** per count; it does not reconcile card payouts. Its daily guidance calls for resolving open orders and appointments. [Register Closeout Entries](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/16239705-register-closeout-entries-report); [Daily Opening & Closing](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941317-daily-opening-closing-procedures). **Proposed safeguard:** a daily list joins cash variance, open orders, unsettled/failed payments and other unresolved work with a responsible person. This combined list is our workflow, not a named Boulevard report.

## Existing app boundary

The approved [contractor financial reconciliation contract](../financial-reconciliation-contract.md) already separates billed sales, payment events, allocation, tax, credits, permissions and accountant exports. The repository has corresponding report readers and a financial export route. This is evidence of a reuse candidate, not proof it fits closed appointment orders, prepaid benefits, product stock, commissions or payout settlement. P3 must assess each before a reuse or readiness claim.

Jafar conditionally agreed the four reporting directions after a Boulevard/established-competitor source check on 2026-10-07. The proposals and gaps above retain their labels; the unit-liability valuation method and delivery order are not settled by that agreement.
