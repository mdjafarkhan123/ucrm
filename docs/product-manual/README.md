# UpliftContractor product manual

This manual explains how UpliftContractor works for the people who use it. It uses everyday
language, practical examples, and step-by-step workflows. No technical knowledge is required.

Choose the area that matches how you use UpliftContractor:

## Platform Owner

The Platform Owner runs the UpliftContractor platform through the private `/jafar` control room.
This includes reviewing contractor applications, creating organizations, controlling commercial
access, helping administrators recover access, and handling platform-level problems.

[Open the Platform Owner manual](platform-owner/README.md)

## Contractors

Contractors use UpliftContractor to manage customers, requests, quotes, jobs, visits, invoices,
payments, team members, and business settings.

[Open the contractor manual](contractor/README.md)

## Customers

Customers use secure pages to request work, review and approve quotes, see appointments, and pay
invoices when those features are available for their contractor.

[Open the customer manual](customer/README.md)

## What this manual includes

A guide is added when its complete user journey has been built, checked in the real product, and is
stable enough for people to use. Each guide explains:

- what that part of the product is for;
- the normal workflow from beginning to end;
- statuses and what they mean;
- actions a person can take and what happens afterward;
- warnings, blocked situations, and safe recovery steps; and
- realistic examples where the correct next step may not be obvious.

Features still being designed or built are not presented as available. This keeps the manual honest
and useful.
