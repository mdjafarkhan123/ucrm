# Classic Job page concept comparison

Two exploratory desktop concepts in `classic.pen`, both using the approved Classic palette and existing shell. They are alternatives for Jafar to compare, not a change to the already approved Job page or product behavior.

| Variant | Pencil frame | What it emphasizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visit first | `Concept A · Job details · Visit-first · 1440` | Today's site visit, checklist, work log, and the immediate work to finish |
| Progress first | `Concept B · Job details · Progress-first · 1440` | The lifecycle, completed visits, current milestone, and job history |

The same sample job, client, value, and profit appear in both. This makes layout and visual hierarchy the choice rather than the sample data. The established Job detail design remains the approved reference until Jafar explicitly selects a replacement.

Reference behavior: [Jobber Job Basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/job-basics/) and [Jobber Visits](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/visits/). Our existing Classic design agreement and `docs/classic-theme-redesign-design-recipe.md` govern colors, component style, and preserved behavior.
