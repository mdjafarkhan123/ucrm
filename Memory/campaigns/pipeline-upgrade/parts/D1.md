## Notes

- Pattern followed: Salesforce/HubSpot stage menu beside dragging, Jira's restrict → validate → act,
  Gmail/Material "Undo" on the confirmation toast (`docs/research/pipeline-blocked-moves-2026-09-21.md`).
- The Move menu lists all seven real stages plus the custom stages of the card's section. Blocked ones stay
  selectable and answer with the reason and a next step; they are never silently disabled.
- Undo exists for five assessment moves and for custom-stage placement. Convert and Send already ask first.
- Undo restores the card's time-in-stage and inactivity clock, so a mistaken move cannot silence a warning.
- Still open after D1: a refused *drag* still snaps back without saying why.
