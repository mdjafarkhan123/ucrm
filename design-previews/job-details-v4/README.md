# V4 — Ink & Copper

Separate desktop palette variant of v3. The approved-for-comparison balanced typography, layout, five visits, sample content, title/instruction editors and photo preview are preserved. Earlier versions remain unchanged.

Navy navigation separates the app shell from the light workspace. Blue owns brand actions and upcoming information. Copper accents distinguish crew guidance and notes; category accents are not statuses. Completed stays green, pending sign-off stays amber, and text labels convey every state. Cool gray layers support the white working panels.

Research: [IBM Carbon color overview](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/color/overview) supports contrasting a dark shell with a light page and using role-based color tokens. [Atlassian color foundations](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color?content_language=English) distinguishes brand buttons, neutral controls, semantic states and nonsemantic accents. This exact palette and composition are our proposal, not a tested product outcome.
