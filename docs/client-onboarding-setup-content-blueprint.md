# Client onboarding — prebuilt setup content

**Status:** Approved by Jafar on 2026-10-03
**Parent contract:** `docs/client-onboarding-delivery-behavior-contract.md` §§ 2–3
**Research:** `docs/research/client-onboarding-setup-content-2026-10-03.md`

## Purpose

Every paid client starts with a complete, useful setup instead of an empty editor. Jafar may edit the draft,
but the default already asks a contractor for the facts and access Uplift needs to configure the CRM and every
purchased service. Common facts are asked once and reused. A stage tied to a service appears only when that
service is in the client's package.

This is an intake brief, not a test. It asks for plain facts and examples; Uplift turns them into settings,
copy, campaigns, and provider submissions. Only facts that genuinely block safe work are required.

## Approved product boundaries (Jafar, 2026-10-03)

- Launch uses one strong universal contractor setup. Examples adapt to the selected trade—General contractor,
  Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Landscaping, Roofing, Painting, Cleaning, Pest control, Handyman, or Other—but
  separate trade packs wait for evidence from real clients.
- Uplift delivers English websites and campaign content at launch. The setup records the customers' preferred
  language; translation or another delivery language is separately scoped.
- Marketing means owned referral and returning-customer email/SMS campaigns. Paid advertising is a separate
  future service, not hidden inside this setup or package promise.
- A Website client without usable branding receives a simple starter wordmark/colour system and approved stock
  imagery. This does not promise a full custom brand identity or photo shoot.
- CRM choices begin with proven recommended defaults for the client to confirm or change. A default that could
  send, publish, charge, import, or make a legal/tax claim stays off until its own explicit approval.
- A new enquiry starts as a Request for review, never an automatically booked Job. The contractor then chooses
  whether it needs an assessment, quote, or direct work.
- Starting money recommendations are: 30-day quote validity, customer approval required, no assumed deposit,
  residential invoices due on receipt, commercial invoices Net 30, and no tax assumption until confirmed.
- Only approved operational email presets may start after sender verification. SMS, review requests, and
  marketing remain off until their own readiness and approval gates pass.
- The client owns the delivered starter wordmark and colour files. Stock imagery remains subject to its licence
  and is never represented as client-owned photography.
- A Marketing package without Calls and texting offers email campaigns only. SMS appears only after Calls and
  texting is included and its sender is ready.
- A mainly non-English audience creates a scope-review task. Ready for Uplift waits until the contractor accepts
  English delivery or separately adds translation.

## Reading the catalogue

- **Required** — needed before Uplift can accept the setup.
- **Required · can defer** — needed eventually, but **I don't have this yet** or **I need Uplift's help** is a
  valid setup answer and creates a visible follow-up.
- **Optional** — useful when it exists; never blocks submission.
- **Conditional** — appears only after the named earlier answer.
- **Reuse** — show the earlier accepted answer for confirmation; do not ask the client to type it again.
- **Structured** — a purpose-built control for hours, addresses, repeatable services/locations/people, or
  uploads; it is not forced into one text box.

Global rules:

1. Prefill facts already supplied during purchase or already present in the CRM, and ask the client to confirm
   or correct them.
2. Ask one question at a time on mobile, group related questions under short headings, autosave, and show an
   honest time estimate per stage.
3. Never ask for a Google, registrar, email, social, hosting, or carrier password. Access is granted by an
   invitation, delegated role, DNS instruction, or a protected one-time task.
4. Country and earlier answers control local wording and provider-only questions. A UK sole trader should not
   see a US EIN question; a contractor who does not buy texting should not see carrier registration.
5. Files show accepted formats and why Uplift needs them. Sensitive provider documents use protected access,
   not ordinary setup attachments.
6. A client may submit with optional items missing. Help/deferred answers remain named follow-ups and do not
   pretend the underlying fact was supplied.
7. The universal question set stays the same across trades, while examples and help text use the selected
   trade's everyday language.

## Stage 4 — How your CRM should work

**Shown to:** everyone. **Outcome:** useful defaults that match the contractor's real day, without asking them
to understand CRM terminology.

Each supported choice opens with Uplift's recommended answer already explained, and the client confirms or
changes it. This shortens setup but never pre-approves sending, publishing, importing, charging, or tax/legal
claims.

Recommended starting values:

- a new enquiry becomes a Request for review, then the contractor chooses assessment, quote, or direct work;
- quotes remain valid for 30 days and require customer approval;
- no deposit or tax treatment is assumed;
- residential invoices are due on receipt and commercial invoices are Net 30; and
- only the operational email presets named below may be prepared for activation after sender verification and
  the contractor's final preview.

## Stage 12 — Check and send to Uplift

**Shown to:** everyone. **Outcome:** one frozen, reviewable brief with honest follow-ups.

The system generates this stage; Jafar does not manually recreate its questions. It shows each applicable
stage with **Complete**, **Needs help**, **Waiting for item**, or **Optional items skipped**, plus an Edit link.
It names every true blocker separately from work Uplift can continue without.

Before **Send to Uplift**, the final approver confirms:

1. the information is accurate to the best of their knowledge;
2. Uplift may use the supplied content, files, photos, testimonials, and data for the agreed work;
3. the person has permission to supply those materials and has disclosed restrictions;
4. Uplift may prepare the purchased services and make approved DNS changes after separate launch approval;
5. the contractor remains owner of the domain and Google profile while Uplift receives delegated access;
6. Uplift may submit truthful phone/text registration and configure the approved routing;
7. an import happens only after a separate preview and approval; and
8. marketing remains a draft until a separate recipient preview and send approval.

The submission records the catalogue version, answers, deferrals/help requests, confirmation wording, final
approver, and time. Publishing a later catalogue version never silently reopens or rewrites this submission.

## Items that need special controls before this catalogue is fully buildable

The ordinary question editor already covers short/long text, one-choice, yes/no, phone, email, date, and
photo/file. These content patterns need purpose-built controls or carefully approved extensions:

- multi-choice and ordered selections;
- repeatable services, locations, people, proof items, links, and message samples;
- structured postal addresses, weekly hours, date exceptions, colours, money/percentage, duration, and
  capacity;
- country-aware labels and provider questions;
- protected sensitive files and access tasks;
- audio upload for voicemail;
- reuse/confirmation of an earlier answer without creating a duplicate fact; and
- stage summaries and final attestations.

Until those controls exist, do not flatten this catalogue into dozens of ambiguous long-text questions merely
to make it fit the current editor.
