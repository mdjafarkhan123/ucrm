## Documented gaps and comparison patterns

Boulevard explicitly describes treatment quotes, invoices and superbills as forms/charts workarounds. It also says forms cannot be sent after a service in that workflow. A workaround is not a formal quote approval, payment-tracking or clinical billing system. [Boulevard forms/chart practices](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15118687-forms-charts-best-practices).

| Gap ID | Boulevard evidence or uncertainty | Established comparison | Proposed direction for our product, pending P2/P3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GAP-01 | Treatment quote form has no dedicated approval/payment tracking | [Zenoti quotes](https://help.zenoti.com/en/point-of-sale/onboard-and-set-up/create-and-manage-quotes.html) documents itemized services/products/packages, expiry, sharing, change history and conversion to an invoice/booking; available on web and iPad ZMA | **Competitor-derived:** assess a proper quote record for consultation-to-purchase journeys. Fits agreed clinical/business scope; assess existing quote capability in P3. Do not assume this is a treatment protocol or automatic clinical approval. |
| GAP-02 | Manual invoice form has no automated taxes, payment or status tracking | [Vagaro invoice checkout](https://support.vagaro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011037574-Create-and-Send-an-Invoice-at-Checkout), updated 2026-03-16, documents itemized invoice, due date, secure payment instructions, drafts and sent/overdue/paid/void states | **Competitor-derived:** evaluate formal unpaid invoices and payment links for remote deposits or unpaid balances. Fits business operations; reuse assessment and release decision remain open. Do not copy a reminder schedule without agreeing it. |
| GAP-03 | Group-booking guide says unnamed guests' forms attach to the main booking client until profiles are assigned | [Vagaro SOAP notes](https://support.vagaro.com/hc/en-us/articles/11954561114523-Fill-Out-a-SOAP-Note-From-a-Customer-s-Profile), updated 2026-04-08, attaches treatment documentation to the selected customer profile | **Competitor-informed proposal:** each treated person should have their own clinical record; a payer or group organizer should not implicitly own it. This source establishes per-person charting, not guardian/proxy permissions. Settle proxy/minor rules separately in P2B. [Boulevard group source](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/7922463-group-booking). |

These comparisons are focused responses to necessary workflow gaps, not a full competitor inventory. Financial or clinical policy is not approved by citing a vendor. Specialist diagnostic systems remain outside the initial promise. Superbills/insurance coding are inventoried as a gap, with necessity and scope still undecided; no diagnostic or insurance system is silently added.
