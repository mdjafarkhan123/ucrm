## Notes

Jafar approved (2026-10-08):
- Calls have a time and a length. Follow-ups have a day; the time is optional.
- **Call defaults:** email 1 day before, plus an in-app alert and an email 15 minutes before.
- **Follow-up defaults:** day-only → in-app at 9:00 that day; with a time → in-app 15 minutes before.
- Defaults are set in My preferences and can be changed per item.
- Reminders go to the person's sign-in email.
- Time zone lives in My preferences and is filled in from the browser.
- A passed call asks how it went: Held, They didn't show (→ "Reschedule" due today), or Cancelled.
- Busy blocks are one-off only.
- The calendar shows sales items only.

Design (Ready): the calendar is one database call per view (at most 42 days). Reminders are derived by triggers, so a move deletes the unsent ones in the same transaction. The worker claims reminders with a 5-minute lease and skips any that are more than 60 minutes late. Verify with 50k Leads and 10k entries seeded: the window read under 50 ms, and the phone LCP/INP.

D3: everything is Jafar's for now (owner columns exist). No area claims the calendar routes yet.
