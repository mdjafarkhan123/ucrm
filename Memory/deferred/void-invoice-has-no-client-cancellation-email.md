# Voiding an invoice tells the client nothing

Deferred by Jafar 2026-09-07 when the Invoices campaign completed.

Void works and is verified, but it sends the client no cancellation notice — there is no template for one.
A customer who already received the invoice email is left holding a bill the contractor has cancelled.

**Reactivates when** invoice email templates are next opened, or a contractor asks why a voided invoice is
still being chased.

Already known: this needs a *new* template, not a variant of an existing one, so it is template work plus
the send, not a wiring change.
