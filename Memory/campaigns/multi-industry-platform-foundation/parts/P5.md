## Proposed build order for Jafar

1. **Experience identity:** Uplift sees and records a reviewed Contractor experience for a test Organization, including decision history.
2. **Package fit:** Uplift can publish and offer only editions whose promises fit the selected experience; incompatible assignment is refused.
3. **One Application:** a buyer describes their work; Uplift can hold unclear or mixed cases and confirm the experience before payment.
4. **Safe provisioning:** a paid, approved Application creates one Organization with its confirmed experience and recoverable administrator invitation.
5. **Access comparison:** Uplift sees old and new Contractor access side by side for real roles and editions before switching anyone.
6. **Workspace access:** eligible Contractor pages, menu, APIs and records agree; Medspa and clinical actions are refused.
7. **Stable Setup:** a Contractor starts a pinned Setup version; later publication cannot rewrite their in-progress answers.
8. **Readiness decisions:** Uplift can review and explain readiness for a named real-world workflow and public surface.
9. **Public continuity:** new forms need release; reviewed existing forms and issued customer links retain their approved safe behavior.
10. **Test migration:** one test Contractor Organization moves with records, access, and customer actions compared before and after; recovery is demonstrated.
11. **Existing Contractor migration:** reviewed Organizations move in small cohorts, stopping on unexplained change and preserving history.
12. **Connected proof:** the full Contractor buyer-to-payment-to-work-to-invoice journey, support and recovery are observed; Medspa work remains gated until it passes.

Each part waits for the relevant prior foundation; migration waits for all access, Setup and public-continuity parts. This is a proposal, not approved implementation scope.
