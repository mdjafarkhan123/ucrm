# Contractor migration and connected proof: P4 evidence

**Scope:** Read-only code and remote database audit on 2026-10-09. The proposed migration is not implemented. Counts are a point-in-time inventory, not proof that these organizations are real customers or ready for public launch. No customer names, contact details or tokens were exported.

## Current facts

- The remote project had **7 Organizations**: 5 `active`, 2 `suspended`; **12 membership rows**: 11 active, 1 removed; **10 Package Agreement rows**: 9 uncancelled, 1 cancelled. An uncancelled row is not necessarily the currently effective Agreement; the migration must resolve effective dates and changes per Organization. The audit also found 1 Setup row, 1 Setup submission, no handover row, and 2 enabled, published forms. These counts came from read-only aggregate queries against the remote project and must be repeated immediately before migration.
- `organizations` currently stores identity and lifecycle status, not Industry experience or Business type. The Application stores applicant `trade` text, but that is not an authoritative classifier. Existing Contractor accounts were created by a Contractor-only product, yet each profile still needs an explicit reviewed migration decision. [P1 code audit](multi-industry-current-state-audit-2026-10-09.md), [generated organization type](../../src/lib/database.types.ts), [Application type](../../src/lib/database.types.ts).
- Effective access is currently assembled from Organization lifecycle, Package Agreement and exceptions, and member grants. Its database snapshot and server resolver do not include experience eligibility. Navigation uses a Contractor-specific resolver. [access snapshot](../../supabase/migrations/20261001090000_package_changes_and_exceptions.sql), [effective access](../../src/lib/server/access/effective.ts), [navigation](../../src/lib/server/access/navigation.ts).
- A published Contractor form can be reachable for an `active` Organization without a Setup handover or a surface-specific launch approval. Existing token pages and APIs separately handle quotes, invoices, job reports, visit actions, reviews, Files and launch approvals. A new public gate must be introduced per journey; a blanket rule based on `active` or handover would change existing behavior. [public form resolver](../../src/lib/server/forms/public-resolver.ts), [customer routes](../../src/routes/(public)/), [public APIs](../../src/routes/api/public/).
- The approved Contractor journey keeps the Application, offsite payment confirmation, provisioned Organization, administrator invitation, Setup, Uplift review, preview, customer approval, Mark as live, support and commercial recovery as separate steps. [delivery contract](../client-onboarding-delivery-behavior-contract.md), [foundation P2–P3](../multi-industry-platform-foundation-behavior-contract.md).
- Jobber's documented Contractor lifecycle keeps Requests, Quotes, Jobs, Visits, Invoices and Payments as distinct linked work records. The migration should preserve those meanings and links rather than translate them into appointment or clinical records. [Jobber reference overview](../../.claude/skills/jobber/jobber-00-overview-lifecycle.md).

## Proposed preservation inventory

The build must take a per-Organization before/after inventory, including row counts, stable identifiers, states, representative reads and externally reachable links. These are the facts to preserve:

| Area | Preserve and compare |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and commercial | Organization ID, slug, lifecycle and suspension reason; application/provisioning link; administrator and staff identities, roles, individual grants and scopes; agreed Package edition and service promises, price, offers, changes, exceptions, allowances, receipts, covered dates and support history. |
| Contractor work | Clients, contacts, properties, Requests, assessments, Quotes and versions/approvals, Jobs, Visits/assignments, invoices, deposits/payments/refunds and their relationships; Files and activity/audit attribution. No Contractor record becomes an Appointment or clinical chart. |
| Configuration and delivery | Branding, settings, connected providers, published forms and versions, automations and queued work, Setup answers/submissions/reviews, provider waits, previews, launch checks/approval, handover and live mark. A missing Setup or handover record stays missing. |
| Customer access | Existing form URLs, quote approval/deposit links, invoice pay links, visit and report links, review and feedback links, shared Files and customer messages/replies, within each link's current expiry, revocation, payment and security rules. Link secrets are never placed in the inventory report. |

The inventory must also list active public surfaces and background senders for each Organization, identify their actual owner and current release evidence, and distinguish a customer obligation already issued from a new public intake or campaign. The two currently published forms require individual review; their publication does not establish that Uplift approved launch.

## Proposed rollout and recovery

1. **Preflight:** repeat the live inventory; resolve current Agreement per Organization; review each Organization's proposed Contractor profile and unknown Business type with Jafar; record affected staff, Packages, Setup, forms, tokens, automations and provider connections. An unclear or mixed clinical business goes through the P2 qualification process and is not silently labelled Contractor.
2. **Add and backfill:** add the experience profile and history without deleting or translating existing rows. Backfill a reviewed Contractor decision for each eligible existing Organization, with migration source and reviewer; keep unconfirmed Business type explicitly unknown. Make the operation repeatable and report missing, duplicate or conflicting profiles before any new access enforcement uses it.
3. **Compare before switching:** run the old Contractor access result and the new experience-aware result side by side for real Organization/role/Package combinations. Compare menu, direct page/API access and record scopes. Any unexplained loss or gain stops that Organization's cutover; the old Contractor path remains in use while corrected.
4. **Roll out in stages:** prove one safe test Organization through the complete journey, then move reviewed existing Organizations in small cohorts. Keep the Contractor shell and customer links working under their current rules until each surface has an explicit readiness and continuity decision. New public surfaces and new customer-intake automation require their own release checks; no new clinical capability is exposed by the Contractor migration.
5. **Recover without erasing history:** if access, records or customer actions diverge, stop the next cohort, restore the prior Contractor resolver for affected Organizations, inspect the audit and repair the cause. Do not roll back by deleting the new profile, rewriting Agreements or discarding customer work. A genuine security issue may require pausing the affected public surface while preserving safe payment, reply and support recovery.

## Observable Contractor proof

The implementation part must verify a fresh paid Contractor journey: Application → Uplift review and agreed edition → recorded payment → provisioned Organization and invitation → Setup and Uplift review → Business Workspace and role-limited menu/direct access → preview and customer approval → released form/request → Quote → Job/Visit → Invoice/payment → support → overdue pause and recovery. Test an existing Organization with its exact agreed edition, historic work and issued customer links before and after cutover. Prove that an unpublished/new surface stays closed, a permitted old obligation remains usable, and direct Medspa/clinical URLs and APIs refuse Contractor access. Record the observed result; this plan alone is not readiness proof.

## Decision for Jafar

**Proposed continuity rule:** an existing active Contractor form that is already enabled and published may continue accepting requests while its Organization remains on the old Contractor path, unless Uplift finds a security or business-safety problem. New or materially changed public forms wait for an explicit surface release. Uplift reviews the existing forms individually before that Organization's cutover and records whether each may continue under the new gate. This preserves a customer's working request link while preventing publication from silently becoming approval in the new model. Jafar must approve this rule and the rollout/recovery plan before P4 closes.
