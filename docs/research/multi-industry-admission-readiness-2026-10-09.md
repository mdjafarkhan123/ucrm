# Admission, readiness and public launch patterns

**Scope:** P2 planning research, 2026-10-09. Official public product guidance only; this does not verify vendor back-end states or Uplift's live readiness.

## What the sources establish

| Moment | Official behavior | P2 relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualification before account setup | Boulevard says its onboarding specialist works with Sales to check business fit **before sign-on**, then helps with service menu, staff, business settings, migration validation and training. [Boulevard onboarding specialist](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/8797012-partnering-with-your-boulevard-onboarding-specialist) | Keep an explicit Uplift decision between Application and provisioning. The public source does not publish Boulevard's acceptance criteria. |
| Stated business shape | Fresha asks for primary and up to three related service types, team size and whether clients visit, staff travel or service is virtual while creating a workspace. [Fresha workspace creation](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/workspace-settings/40-create-a-new-workspace) | Collect enough facts to route an Application to human review. A buyer's selected label alone cannot prove clinical suitability; this last sentence is Uplift's inference. |
| Account setup versus booking | Boulevard says payment and bank details are required for account activation; online booking then needs the self-booking overlay. Its new-location checklist specifically instructs businesses to leave location booking disabled until ready for go-live. [Boulevard payment setup](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941336-getting-started-payment-setup); [new-location launch checklist](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/11326035-checklist-adding-additional-locations-to-your-dashboard) | Treat paid/access-enabled, configured, and customer-facing availability as separate facts. This does **not** prove Boulevard uses one universal launch approval state. |
| Booking-specific checks | Boulevard requires a service enabled at the location, assigned staff and available staff schedules before online booking can populate. A staff member or service may be unavailable online while still usable for internal appointments. [Boulevard online booking controls](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941347-enabling-and-disabling-online-booking) | Readiness and public permission may vary by surface, location, service and staff. Do not use an organization `active` flag alone as booking permission. |
| Independent publication | Fresha's workspace can exist before the owner publishes its marketplace profile and enables online booking after entering business details and reviewing terms/billing. Square similarly offers booking enable/disable plus site preview/edit and Publish. [Fresha profile publication](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/academy/get-booked-online/accept-online-bookings/lessons/100348); [Square online booking setup](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5355-set-up-online-booking-with-square-appointments) | A business workspace and a customer-facing launch are distinct user-visible moments across products. Their exact controls differ. |
| Contractor comparison | Jobber distinguishes basic account setup from advanced setup, which includes online booking and which services and availability to offer. [Jobber basic setup](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/first-steps-basic-account-set-up/); [Jobber advanced setup](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/first-steps-advanced-account-set-up/) | Contractor should retain its own readiness checks; clinical checks should not be imposed on it merely because Medspa has them. |

## Proposed Uplift translation for P2 discussion

1. **Assisted admission:** Let the Application capture the buyer's stated work, location/state, contact and primary services, then let Uplift confirm a supported Industry experience and Business type, record who made the decision, and hold unclear or mixed cases for review. This is a Uplift policy proposal, not a documented Boulevard form or algorithm. The existing [business-entry decision](boulevard-business-entry-onboarding-2026-10-07.md) already requires owner-reviewed transitions and holds ambiguous clinical cases.
2. **Separate meanings:** Record commercial/account access, administrator invitation, Setup progress, operational readiness review, and customer-facing permission separately. Payment can open Setup under the approved Uplift model, but it cannot stand in for launch approval. Preserve the existing delivery approval evidence and define the exact gate for each public form or booking surface.
3. **Explain the hold:** In the Business Workspace, show which concrete task remains (for example, service/staff availability or a required Uplift review), who can finish it, and whether internal work is allowed. In the Control Room, show the decision and evidence before enabling a public surface. This display design is an inference from the separated vendor steps, not a vendor feature claim.
4. **No blanket readiness claim:** A launch check for one public request or booking surface does not prove a clinic is clinically, legally or operationally ready. Medspa-specific gates need the approved Medspa contract and first-clinic review before go-live.

## Unresolved evidence and decisions

- Public Boulevard documentation does not provide its complete sales qualification rubric, a formal business-type classification decision record, or the back-end state machine for admission and launch. Do not attribute Uplift's proposed states to Boulevard.
- P2 must choose the minimum Application questions, exact buyer-visible waiting/correction language, decision owner, reassignment process, and whether each public surface has a separate release decision or shares an approved launch gate.
- The [P1 audit](multi-industry-current-state-audit-2026-10-09.md) found that Uplift's current `active` organization can already serve a published form while delivery launch remains separate. A code and live-data inventory is needed before changing that rule for existing Contractor businesses.

**Reference method:** Boulevard supplies the primary assisted-onboarding and booking evidence; Fresha and Square clarify public publication, and Jobber supplies the Contractor comparison. These are product behavior analogies, not proof of shared architecture or clinic readiness.
