# Boulevard connected journeys — P2G research

**Checked:** 2026-10-07. **Status:** Official public-source findings and approved product rules. Sources establish published vendor behavior, not that our app already supports an integration or migration. Jafar approved the three recommended boundaries on 2026-10-07. Release assignments remain P3.

## Connections and one booking record

- **Boulevard evidence:** A custom app declares only the scopes it needs, gives a reason for each, and is installed by a business; its newer guide limits custom apps to Enterprise. OAuth and offline access differ. API availability does not mean every partner or action is available on every plan. [App registration](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/17182228-creating-and-registering-your-app-with-boulevard); [Admin API authentication](https://developers.joinblvd.com/2026-06/admin-api/authentication).
- **Boulevard evidence:** Website/social/Google booking entry points lead into Boulevard booking. Personal calendar sync can lag, has a two-month window and unsupported recurring imports; events must be edited at their origin. [Facebook and Instagram](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941546-facebook-instagram-book-now-app); [Reserve with Google](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/6790178-reserve-with-google-integration); [personal calendar](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941554-personal-calendar-syncing).
- **Approved rule:** Each connection has an owner-approved purpose, allowed data and actions, and visible last success or failure. The CRM appointment remains authoritative for booking, eligibility, reminders and checkout. An external calendar can block time or show a neutral appointment, but delayed sync cannot be presented as a guaranteed live booking. Ordinary business apps receive no charts, forms, photos, treatment details or minor-confidential records. Disconnecting stops future access; staff must be told that data already copied to the other provider needs separate handling. These are our safeguards, not claimed Boulevard implementation details.

## Migration and exit

- **Boulevard evidence:** Its export guide lists bulk business reports, balances, client lists and appointments. Client notes, appointment notes, forms, charts and photos require individual saving. New and old reporting suites differ; payouts are exported per payout. [Export and backup](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/17176434-exporting-backing-up-your-data-from-boulevard).
- **Evidence gap:** The [historical-client-files guide](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/11584912-historical-client-files-export-and-import-guide) links a 13.4 MB PDF. Its detailed format was not verified: the web reader rejected its size and direct download was unavailable in this research environment. Do not promise bulk clinical-history migration or a particular import format from this page.
- **Approved rule:** Before a clinic starts using imported records, show a sample, matched and unmatched people, field mappings, counts, future appointments, and prepaid balances for authorized staff to review and approve. Keep source and import date visible. An unmatched or conflicting record stays in a review queue rather than silently merging. Imported consent is usable only with its valid evidence, as agreed in P2D. Historic clinical files stay identified as history; a qualified clinician reviews anything relied on for current care, and import alone never creates clearance, chart sign-off or a current prescription. Export and exit must identify which data can be exported in bulk and which requires manual handling. Migration support for each source vendor needs a tested sample before being promised.
- **Specialist comparison:** Zenoti keeps gift-card purchase, redemption and remaining balance traceable, and separately exposes imported membership-credit expiry corrections. This supports reviewing balances and history rather than importing a single unverified total. [Zenoti gift cards](https://help.zenoti.com/en/appointments/manage-guest-experience/manage-gift-cards.html); [membership service credits](https://help.zenoti.com/en/appointments/manage-guest-experience/manage-memberships/manage-membership-service-credits.html).

## Mobile and later locations

- **Boulevard evidence:** The Professional app is a companion with surface-specific limits; mobile checkout lacks some voucher, loyalty, group, retail-only and order-management actions. The Duo customer-facing iPad and card-reader journey is separate. A phone app or terminal is therefore not proof of complete web parity. [Professional app](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/9180930-professional-app); [mobile checkout](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941513-professional-app-checkout); [Duo](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941542-boulevard-duo-introduction).
- **Approved boundary:** Any surface we do offer follows the same person identity, staff permission, clinical privacy and financial rules. It must say when an action requires another supported surface. P3 decides responsive web, native companion and hardware release assignments separately; no native app or card-present terminal is promised by P2G.
- **Boulevard evidence:** A business can share client profiles across locations while operating schedules, permissions and some membership settings by location. [Client profiles](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941460-client-profiles); [multi-location](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/11682178-multilocation-business-support); [membership plans](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/10012451-memberships-creating-a-membership-plan).
- **Approved later boundary:** One person may have a shared business identity, but appointments, staff access, service prices, stock, payments and benefits retain a clear location. Cross-location prepaid use requires a clinic policy and financial trail before it is enabled. Zenoti's cross-center redemption reporting illustrates the separate purchase and use locations and resulting balances; it is a comparison, not an initial-release promise. [Zenoti cross-center redemptions](https://help.zenoti.com/en/reports/finance/accounting/cross-center-redemptions-report--v1-.html). The first release remains single-location.

## Cross-area checks carried into P3

1. A website or social booking enters the same availability, identity, clearance, deposit, reminder and staff queue rules as an in-app booking; an external calendar delay cannot double-book silently.
2. A migrated client with a duplicate name, old chart, prepaid balance and past text opt-out cannot automatically gain clearance, spend unverified value or receive marketing.
3. A retail/stock or accounting connection shows conflicting quantities or failed exports for staff to resolve; it cannot alter a completed sale or invent collected money. Clinical stock lot-to-patient trace stays protected.
4. A future second location does not turn shared identity into unrestricted chart access or automatic cross-location benefit redemption.

**P3 feasibility checks:** Select actual initial integrations and mobile surfaces; verify provider availability, privacy agreements, data classes, sync direction, failure recovery and reconciliation with the existing app. Confirm each source vendor's export quality with a sample. Multi-location remains later.
