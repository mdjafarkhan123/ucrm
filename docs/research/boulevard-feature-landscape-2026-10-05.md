## Availability snapshot

The rendered [pricing comparison](https://www.joinblvd.com/pricing) was checked for both Salon & Spa and Medspa. Salon Essentials allows five bookable professionals; Medspa Aesthetics Essentials allows three. Higher tiers list unlimited professionals. Medspa plans include forms, photo markup and chart sign-off; salon plans vary between included and add-on access. Shared memberships and multi-location tools appear above Essentials. Marketing allowances, storage, transaction charges and add-ons remain plan-dependent. This snapshot is not a purchase quote or our proposed subscription structure.

Older help articles conflict with pricing or newer releases on some entitlements and access terms. Those conflicts remain explicit in the gap register. Prescribing, financing, AI reception, hardware and partner integrations have additional restrictions; their inventory entries preserve these.

## Findings that affect planning

- Clinical records need careful person-level identity: Boulevard's unnamed group-guest forms initially attach to the booking client. The competitor-informed alternative is recorded for P2, not silently adopted.
- Treatment quotes, invoices and superbills are documented as forms/charts workarounds. Focused Zenoti and Vagaro comparisons establish alternatives for evaluation.
- Service vouchers, money credit and prepaid product units are different. Their redemption, inventory and accounting behavior must stay distinct.
- Beau's documented call workflow sends a booking link; it does not establish completed voice bookings. Booking-time financing promotion does not establish online BNPL checkout.
- Mobile, desktop, partner and multi-location capabilities differ. Report rollout and old/new permissions terminology also require reconciliation.

The linked entries and gap register contain the primary evidence for these findings. No private architecture, medical suitability or implementation readiness is inferred.
