# Borders

- Cards, tables, panels: 1px `--color-border`.
- Fields: 1px `--color-border--interactive`.
- White buttons (secondary, danger, menu, icon, joined groups): no border; their edge is the `--shadow-button--key`
  ring. A switched-on button adds a 1px `--color-interactive` border.
- Table rows and the sidebar's right edge: 1px `--color-border`.
- Field error: 1.5px `--color-critical`.
- Focus: `--shadow-focus` ring.
