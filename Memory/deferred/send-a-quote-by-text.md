# Send a quote by text message

**Why it waits:** A quote can be emailed today but not texted. The Pipeline send window (pipeline-upgrade part B3) therefore offers only Email and "sent outside UCRM". Texting a quote belongs to the Quotes area and needs live SMS, which waits on Jafar's business registration (communications-activation).
**Brings it back:** SMS goes live, or Jafar asks for quote texting. Build it in the Quotes area, then add Text as a third choice in the Pipeline send window.
**Known constraints:** Jobber's drop prompt offers email or text message. Texting must obey Communications consent, opt-out, and allowance rules, and a queued text counts as sent the same way a queued email does in the plan's § Movement and automation.
