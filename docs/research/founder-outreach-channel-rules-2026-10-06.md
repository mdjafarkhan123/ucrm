# Founder outreach: channel rules (6 October 2026)

Primary sources checked for Uplift Contractor's own prospecting. These rules can change; check the linked source again before automating a channel or scaling volume. This is a source summary, not a legal opinion about every recipient's location.

| Channel | What the source says | Practical implication for Uplift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram and Facebook/Messenger | Meta says its community rules apply across these products. Its Instagram guidance warns against repetitive comments/content and repeatedly contacting people for commercial purposes without consent; repeated unwanted messages can be removed. [Meta/Instagram community guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/help/477434105621119?locale=en_GB) | Research a company, write a personal message when genuinely relevant, and stop if ignored or declined. Do not build a bulk DM sender or treat repeated follow-ups as safe. This is our conservative inference; Meta does not publish a safe daily DM number here. |
| Google Maps | The current Maps Platform terms prohibit exporting or scraping Maps content for use outside the service, including copying/saving business names, addresses, or reviews. Consumer Maps terms also prohibit copying content and mass downloads/bulk feeds. [Maps Platform terms, section 3.2.3](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms), [Maps end-user terms](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/) | Use Maps to discover a business manually, then verify its contact information on its own website or another independently licensed source before recording it. Do not build a Maps scraper, import Maps listings into `/jafar`, or use Places API output as a prospect database. This workflow recommendation is an inference from the cited restrictions. |
| US commercial email | CAN-SPAM applies to business-to-business email, including a single promotional email. The FTC requires accurate headers and subject lines, ad disclosure, a valid postal address, an easy opt-out, and honoring opt-outs within 10 business days. The opt-out method must work for at least 30 days after sending. [FTC compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) | A personal cold email to a US business is not automatically exempt. Before sending, Uplift needs a valid postal address and a reliable suppression/opt-out process. Recipient location may trigger other rules. |
| Gmail delivery | Gmail requires SPF or DKIM for all senders to personal Gmail accounts, and SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for senders at 5,000+ messages/day. It requires spam rates below 0.3%; bulk marketing must support one-click unsubscribe. Google advises against sending to people who did not sign up, buying addresses, and abrupt volume increases. [Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en), [sender FAQ](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414?hl=en) | Verify `upliftcontractor.com` authentication before using its email for outreach. More inboxes do not solve poor targeting or recipient complaints. CAN-SPAM compliance alone does not guarantee Gmail delivery. |
| WhatsApp Business | Current policy allows contacting a person only when they have given their number or username **and** opted in to subsequent WhatsApp messages/calls. Businesses must respect opt-outs and avoid spam or surprise messages. The Business App terms also prohibit unsolicited electronic communications. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/), [Business App terms](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/WhatsApp-Terms-for-WhatsApp-Business-App) | Do not cold-message numbers found on websites or Maps via WhatsApp. Use it after a prospect asks to continue there or gives clear permission, and record that permission. |

## Product controls suggested by these rules

These are design inferences, not vendor requirements: record where each contact detail came from; record channel permission and opt-outs; block sends to suppressed contacts; leave social DMs and WhatsApp as human-led tasks until a compliant integration is justified; and keep a human review step before each first contact.
