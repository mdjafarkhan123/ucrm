## Notes

- Pattern followed: Salesforce/HubSpot stage menu beside dragging, Jira's restrict → validate → act,
  Gmail/Material "Undo" on the confirmation toast (`docs/research/pipeline-blocked-moves-2026-09-21.md`).
- The Move menu lists all seven real stages plus every custom stage. Blocked ones stay selectable and
  answer with the reason and a next step; they are never silently disabled.
- Undo exists for five assessment moves and for custom-stage placement, for two minutes, by the person who
  moved it. Convert and Send already ask first. Undo restores time-in-stage and the inactivity clock.
- For stage F reports: an Undo leaves two stage events (the move and its reverse); time-in-stage and
  funnel numbers should not count that pair.
- Still open after D1: a refused *drag* still snaps back without saying why.
- `npm run check` needs `NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=8192` or it runs out of memory.
- `supabase/migrations/20261002203000_client_setup_draft_answers.sql` belongs to the onboarding session.
