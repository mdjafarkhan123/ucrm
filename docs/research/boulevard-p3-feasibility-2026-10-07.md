# Boulevard P3 — reuse and feasibility checkpoint

**Status:** Research and recommendation, 2026-10-07. Release assignments remain open. Public vendor documents describe vendor behavior, not readiness of our product or a clinic's account.

## First-party provider and migration evidence

- **Cards and payouts.** [Stripe SetupIntents](https://docs.stripe.com/api/setup_intents/create) and [future-use payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-during-payment) support saved-card consent and later off-session charges; a decline or required authentication still needs a recovery path. Our [current payment-key guide](../online-payments-behavior-contract.md) and `src/lib/server/payments/stripe-connection.ts` support invoice/quote Checkout, not that flow. Stripe's [payout API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payouts/list) and [balance transactions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/list) can explain processor fees and net payout. Payout-linked balance transaction filtering requires automatic payouts, and [`payout.paid`](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/types) is not proof the clinic's bank confirmed arrival. More key permissions and a clinic-specific Stripe test are needed. [Stripe's restricted-business terms](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) also make service-specific eligibility a check, not an assumption.
- **Prescribing.** [DoseSpot Jumpstart](https://dosespot.com/jumpstart/) and [Full Integration](https://dosespot.com/full-integration/) are plausible qualified-provider routes. Commercial access, clinical data terms, enrollment and state coverage are unverified. [DEA EPCS guidance](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/faq/epcs-faq.html) describes identity and two-factor controls for controlled-substance prescribing. No prescribing release promise follows.
- **Historical files.** Boulevard's [linked two-page guide](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/11584912-historical-client-files-export-and-import-guide) was checked visually: historical forms, charts, photos and videos may be brought in as **files** when every filename contains the unique client ID. Secure transfer and staff handling are required; this does not convert them into current structured charts or clearance. Boulevard's newer [export guide](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/17176434-exporting-backing-up-your-data-from-boulevard) says forms, charts and photos must be saved individually from profiles, not bulk-exported. Source-vendor samples remain necessary before any import promise.
- **Old value.** Boulevard's [export guide](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/17176434-exporting-backing-up-your-data-from-boulevard) lists client, appointment, membership, gift-card, voucher and credit reports, but exportability does not prove a correct import into our different ledger. Its [Shopify gift-card guidance](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/5941544-shopify-integration) explicitly converts older cards and disables the source card to prevent double spending. Our recommendation is reviewed, source-specific mapping and totals; a clinic with unresolved paid obligations must not be told its migration is complete.
- **Clinical service readiness.** [Zenoti](https://help.zenoti.com/en/inventory/operational-tasks/batch-and-expiry-management-in-inventory.html) and [Aesthetic Record](https://learn.aestheticrecord.com/en/articles/9247099-add-traceability-information-to-a-procedure) link injectable lot, expiry and amount to the treated person and procedure. Boulevard [acknowledges](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/10015766-packages) that product-unit package checkout does not track stock. The approved operations plan permits an explicitly external clinical stock record until our full trace works; the app must not imply it manages stock it does not record.
- **Children and groups.** Boulevard's [optional group booking](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/7922463-group-booking) can leave a guest without a profile and attach forms to the booking client. Our approved person-level clinical record rule avoids that gap. A group feature needs each treated person's identity and record. A clinic treating minors needs the complete approved representative and confidentiality behavior before that journey is offered; public vendor behavior does not set one rule for every state.
- **Protected information.** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/hipaa-projects) says PHI projects require a signed BAA and HIPAA add-on. [Cloudflare's HIPAA paper](https://www.cloudflare.com/resources/assets/slt3lc6tev37/3PeVHvuZAh7p3tN77kIfhG/596a459dd1cfbc2ad6b1dbef36ff0eb1/BDES-1265_Privacy_Compliance_Whitepapers_HIPAA.pdf) describes Enterprise BAA availability but does not establish coverage of our R2 account. [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/docs/iam/twilio-editions/hippa) describes eligible services, account edition, BAA and workflow requirements. Actual agreements and settings for every PHI processor, including hosting, mail, storage, logs and backups, are unverified. This is a clinical launch gate, not a conclusion that the present stack is compliant.

## Corrected release recommendation for Jafar to review

Do not divide the market into “new clinics” and “existing clinics” by default. Start with one complete booking-to-follow-up clinical journey and a **first-clinic eligibility review**: its offered treatments, age range, future bookings, paid benefits, source data and stock practice must match capabilities that are actually verified. Use assisted migration with reviewed matches and totals. Historical files remain attributed attachments until a clinician reviews relevance. No migration grants marketing consent, treatment clearance or a signed chart.

Core booking, person-level intake/consent/chart/clearance, appointment checkout, neutral notices, staff access, audit and a small reconciled report set belong together. Sequencing groups, guardian access, stored-value programs, injectable stock or integrations later is legitimate only when the first clinic does not depend on them, or an explicit, reviewed outside process safely covers the gap. Boulevard treats [medspa packages](https://support.boulevard.io/en/articles/15117321-packages-best-practices) as real business offers; their release timing cannot be decided by assuming every clinic can operate without them. Optional native apps, AI, financing and multi-location are separate later candidates.

The owner still needs a concrete recommendation for the **first clinic and treatment profile** after this feasibility work, then approval of initial/later scope in P4. P3 should assign each initial capability a reuse/new decision and an observable completion check. P3A sets measurable performance workloads and budgets. No application, provider or infrastructure change is authorized by this note.
