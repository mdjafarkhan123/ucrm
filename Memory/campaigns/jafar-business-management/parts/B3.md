## Notes

Jafar, 2026-10-07 (all recommended options):
- A **review queue**: one card per business — why it fits, where each detail was found, country, past contact, missing info, exclusions — approve chosen details or send back, then the next card.
- **Do not contact** covers the whole business; records who and when; only undone on purpose.
- Approving makes the Lead's next action "First contact: …", due today (changeable), owned by the Lead's owner; logging outbound contact ticks it off.
- WhatsApp is blocked as "Needs their permission first" unless "They asked to talk on WhatsApp" is ticked; that permission goes into history.

Design choices (not Jafar's): approving and undoing do-not-contact are a new sensitive action in Leads; "already a client" = linked Application paid or account created; editing an approved detail's text or removing it withdraws its approval; a manual status change away from Approved withdraws approvals; opt-out on an Approved Lead makes it Unsuitable.
