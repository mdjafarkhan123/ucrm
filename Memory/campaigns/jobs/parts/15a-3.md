# Part 15a-3: the Schedule asks a field worker for their own visits

Opened by the 15a-2 performance gate, which found this and did not fix it. Not yet approved — the fix is a
product decision about what the Schedule shows a field worker, so present it to Jafar before building.

## The measurement that opened it

A 5,000-job tenant, a field worker assigned to 500 of them, one week of Schedule:

| Read | Before 15a-2 | 15a-2 as first written | After the single-statement fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jobs list, 25 rows | 35 ms | 63 ms | **24 ms** |
| Schedule, one week | 27 ms | 938 ms | **132 ms** |

The 938 ms was `can_view_job` nesting four SECURITY DEFINER calls, none of which the planner inlines, once
per scanned row. Flattening it to one statement is committed and closed that gap.

The remaining 27 → 132 ms is structural, not per-row. The plan says `Rows Removed by Filter: 786`: the
Schedule asks for the whole organization's week and RLS throws away what this worker may not see. The cost
therefore scales with the tenant's visits, not with the worker's own — the wrong quantity for a 40,000-user
target.

## The shape of the fix

When the caller's `jobs.view` scope is `'assigned'`, the Schedule query should be driven by their
assignments instead of the org's calendar, so the index selects the ~90 rows rather than scanning 876 and
discarding 786. `src/routes/api/schedule/visits/+server.ts` is the read; `job_visit_assignments
(organization_id, user_id, job_id)` shipped in 15a-2 is the index.

Jobber calls this "My Schedule" for a field user, which is the product question to put to Jafar: does a
field worker's Schedule become their own by default, and does anything let them see the wider calendar?

## Do not redo

- The narrowing itself is correct and verified live; this is only about how the Schedule asks for it.
- `private.can_view_job` is deliberately one flat statement duplicating `has_permission` / `permission_scope`
  for `jobs.view`. Do not "clean it up" back into nested calls — that is the 938 ms.
