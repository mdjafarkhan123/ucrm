Decisions (Jafar, 2026-10-07; plan updated): confirming payment on the linked Application makes the open Deal Won by itself, and linking an already-paid Application does too, with no "Mark as Won" button · a payment reversed after Won leaves the Deal Won with a red "Payment reversed" warning · Won leaves the board and sits behind a "Won" button, like Lost · setup is looked after by Jafar by default, with a Change button offering teammates who have Onboarding access, recorded in the history.

My design choice: a Deal records which Application won it (one Deal per Application), so a later new Deal for the same client isn't marked Won by the old payment.
