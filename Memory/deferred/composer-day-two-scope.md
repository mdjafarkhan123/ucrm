# Composer follow-up scope

- **Priority:** P2
- **Why postponed:** Outbound CC, rich formatting/emoji, and complete channel switching require real provider, validation, allowance, or rendering support. Outbound attachments are already shipped.
- **Reactivate when:** The composer is intentionally expanded or another channel becomes ready.
- **Constraint:** Unavailable channels need actionable reasons; do not ship decorative controls without working behavior.
- **Pointer:** docs/unified-inbox-behavior-contract.md.
